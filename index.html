Frequently asked questions

Quick answers on GST, income tax, TDS and compliance.

This is general information. We confirm the current rules and limits for your case before we act.

Ask your question
Who needs to register for GST?

Businesses whose turnover crosses the prescribed limit need to register, and some activities require registration regardless of turnover. Limits vary by state and type of supply, so we check the current rules for your business before registering.

How often are GST returns filed?

Regular taxpayers generally file GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B every month, while eligible small taxpayers can opt for quarterly filing. An annual return is also required. We track your due dates and file on time.

Who has to file an income tax return?

Anyone whose income is above the basic exemption limit must file. Some people need to file even below it, such as those with high-value transactions, foreign assets or a refund to claim. Filing on time also helps with loans, visas and refunds.

What documents are needed for income tax filing?

Usually PAN, Aadhaar, bank statements, Form 16 or other income details, interest certificates, and proofs of investments and deductions. Tell us your situation and we will give you an exact list.

What is TDS and when is it deposited?

TDS is tax deducted by the payer when paying salary, rent, professional fees, contractor bills and similar payments. It is generally deposited with the government by the 7th of the following month, and quarterly TDS returns are filed.

Who must register for ESIC and EPFO?

Employers must register once their number of employees and wage levels cross the prescribed limits. We check whether your business is covered and handle registration, monthly payments and returns.

Which licences can you help with?

Trade License, Professional Tax (P Tax), Fire License, MSME registration and their renewals. The documents depend on the licence, so call us and we will guide you.
